# Jspark Short — Edit Plan

## Objective

Turn the strongest contrarian insight from the source into a 53.5-second, 9:16 educational Short that feels native to Jspark's academic-comeback positioning.

## Visual system

- 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, 53.5 seconds.
- Center-weighted editorial crop with a restrained punch-in reset at each jump cut.
- Ink-black, warm paper, white, and highlighter yellow palette; sky blue is reserved for progress indicators.
- Two-line captions above the Shorts UI safe zone. Key words receive physical highlighter blocks.
- Motion graphics: red strike-through on the opening premise, exam-paper checklist, answer-vs-mark-scheme meter, and final stamped takeaway.
- Short white cut flashes and low-volume click/scribble accents only at structural beats.
- Mild vignette, contrast normalization, and line texture unify mixed footage without an artificial “template” look.

## Audio

- Preserve and normalize the original voice.
- Use the existing source bed where present.
- Keep supplemental sound effects below dialogue and limit them to the opening strike and three edit points.

## Export

H.264 MP4, 1080 × 1920, CRF 18, AAC 320 kbps.
